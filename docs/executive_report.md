# Executive Report: AI Marketing Campaign Intelligence

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze multi-channel marketing campaign performance and provide practical recommendations for budget allocation, campaign optimization, and audience targeting.

## Data Used

The dataset is synthetic and created for portfolio purposes. It represents realistic campaign performance across channels, cities, objectives, and audience segments.

Data fields include:

- Campaign date
- Campaign name
- Marketing channel
- City
- Audience segment
- Campaign objective
- Spend
- Impressions
- Clicks
- Leads
- Conversions
- Revenue
- Customer rating

## Methodology

1. Generated a realistic marketing campaign dataset.
2. Cleaned and validated the dataset using Python.
3. Created calculated KPIs such as CTR, CPC, conversion rate, CPA, ROAS, and average order value.
4. Aggregated performance by channel, campaign, city, audience segment, and week.
5. Created charts for executive review.
6. Converted findings into business recommendations.

## Key Insights

1. Retargeting produced the strongest return, generating approximately AUD 782K revenue from AUD 54K spend, with a ROAS of 14.44.
2. Email was the most efficient low-cost channel, generating approximately AUD 342K revenue from AUD 24K spend, with a ROAS of 14.39.
3. Google Search was the strongest scalable acquisition channel, generating approximately AUD 1.19M revenue with a ROAS of 7.87.
4. Melbourne and Bangalore were the strongest markets by commercial impact, with Melbourne generating the highest revenue and Bangalore producing the highest city-level ROAS.
5. LinkedIn Ads and Influencer campaigns showed weaker direct ROAS, meaning they should be optimized, narrowed to stronger audiences, or measured as upper-funnel channels.

## Recommendations

1. Increase incremental budget for retargeting, email, and Google Search because they show the strongest revenue efficiency.
2. Keep influencer and LinkedIn campaigns active only where they support brand awareness, premium partnerships, or remarketing audience creation.
3. Build city-specific campaign playbooks for Melbourne, Bangalore, and Sydney instead of using one generic strategy across all markets.
4. Create weekly executive dashboards for spend, revenue, conversion rate, ROAS, and CPA.
5. Use AI-assisted summaries to convert dashboard outputs into faster management reports and clearer recommendations.

## Business Impact

This project demonstrates how raw campaign data can become an executive decision system. It connects technical analytics with commercial action, which is critical for analytics, consulting, and business intelligence roles.
