# Dashboard Wireframe

Build this dashboard in Power BI or Tableau using the processed CSV files.

## Page 1: Executive Overview

KPI cards:

- Total revenue
- Total spend
- ROAS
- Conversions
- CPA
- Conversion rate

Charts:

- Revenue by channel
- ROAS by channel
- Weekly revenue and spend trend
- Top 10 campaigns by revenue

## Page 2: Channel and Campaign Performance

Filters:

- Date
- Channel
- City
- Objective
- Audience segment

Charts:

- Campaign scatter plot: spend vs revenue, sized by conversions
- Channel table with spend, revenue, ROAS, CPA, CTR, conversion rate
- Campaign ranking table

## Page 3: Audience and Market Opportunity

Charts:

- Audience segment ROAS
- City revenue map or bar chart
- Lead-to-conversion rate by segment
- Recommended budget action by channel

## Design Notes

- Use a clean executive style.
- Keep the first page suitable for a senior manager.
- Use blue for revenue, green for ROAS, red for high CPA warnings.
- Add one recommendation text box per page.
